## Q2 — Cooldown

Measured from last OPEN/REOPEN, not recovery or delivery receipt. First OPEN at UTC 00:00:02. After restore, three fresh bad samples ended at last alert +899/+900/+901 seconds. No notifications are sent by this source.

| Prior state | 899 s | 900 s | 901 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OPEN | SUPPRESSED_COOLDOWN, seq 1 | REOPEN, seq 2 | REOPEN, seq 2 |
| RECOVERED | SUPPRESSED_COOLDOWN, stays RECOVERED, seq 1 | OPEN, seq 2 | OPEN, seq 2 |

Suppressed events retain the prior dedupe key; new events use sequence 2 and a distinct key. **F2:** Cooldown suppresses the state transition after RECOVERED and resets the bad streak, so crossing 900 seconds alone does not open the incident. Under continuing bad health it also increments incident sequence at 900 seconds without a recovery. Source/report describe cooldown as notification-distinct yet associate it with incident state/identity. Pin policy owner decision: if notification-only, keep incident lifecycle/sequence independent and model notification eligibility separately. If cooldown intentionally gates incident creation, document the discarded streak and persistent OPEN identity explicitly, then assert those decisions.
